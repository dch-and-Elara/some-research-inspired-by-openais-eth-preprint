# AI disclosure

Chuhang Ding set the research questions and direction and proposed conjectures. AI assistants and agents contributed to proofs, tests, Lean formalization, review, and writing.

This statement applies to all seven papers listed in the [README](README.md). Machine checks and internal reviews do not constitute independent human peer review. No claim of research priority is made here.

## Additional details for the Boolean-cube paper

The project owner supplied the v0.2 manuscript of *Optimal Degrees for Local Polynomial Reconstruction on the Boolean Cube*. It records human–AI collaboration in formulating the problem and developing its proofs. AI systems also contributed to the mathematical review, the Lean implementation, theorem-by-theorem checks, and preparation of this release.

For the unrestricted revision the project owner supplied a review proposing the input-coordinate square certificate and an extension to bilinear tests. The assistant checked the orientation of the two inputs, developed the explicit independence proof in Section 7.2, implemented the Lean proofs and prepared the revised exposition.

The formal proofs were checked with each paper’s pinned toolchain. See the theorem indexes and verification records linked in the README for the scope of those checks.
