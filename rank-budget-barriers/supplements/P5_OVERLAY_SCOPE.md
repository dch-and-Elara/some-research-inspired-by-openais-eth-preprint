## Closed source matches

- **§11 / supplemental packing theorem.** `eventual_every_collision_packing_root`
  first chooses the threshold from the expansion constant, then accepts
  each sufficiently large finite nonempty loopless cubic graph, its given
  boundary expansion, and its monic source-degree, mod-2-irreducible
  polynomial. The actual physical `P` may be any packing satisfying
  `CollisionBound`, not only a greedy output. One ready root menu for
  that same `P` witnesses both the cardinal bound and the literal
  `rootVisitLedger` length for every input pin word. A correct count
  and the ledger lower bound also coexist in
  `eventual_collision_root_same_agenda_count_and_visits`; the integral
  lift of the same `publicMenuSum` reduces at `4N+1` to the actual
  cloned CNF count. Neither result adds a bit-clock.
- **§2.3 error / width examples.** Four roots in the actual residue
  ring `ZMod 8` coexist with degree two and a unit anchor. For the
  actual right interpolation cardinal of the centers `0,2^p`, its
  anchor coefficient has two-adic valuation `-p`; the nonzero datum
  is indistinguishable from zero at precision `p`. Entrywise least
  representatives mod three convert the concrete outer-product
  integral lift into a matrix with determinant -3 and rational rank
  two. These are formal exact ring and scalar-polynomial facts.
- **§2.3 insufficiency outside the sampled grid.** The two distinct
  inputs `0` and the actual product `allocated s k` give equal scaled
  Hermite data for every allocated order, but different evaluations
  at any anchor not in `s`. Hence under the inclusive degree cap
  `sum k ≤ d` no decoder at all can recover the anchor, not only no
  fixed linear decoder.
- **§2.3 determinant and minor counterexamples.** Over an arbitrary
  field, the actual `diag(z,z)` over `K[z]/z²` has determinant zero,
  needs at least two factors' intermediate coordinates in any quotient
  factorization, and admits width exactly two. Every 2×2 minor of
  `TI₃` is divisible by `T²`, but the determinant has order exactly
  three at zero and is not divisible by `T⁴`.
- **§2.3 singularity and residue-anchor examples.** The concrete
  `diag(1,0)` has a zero column, zero polynomial determinant and rank
  one after evaluation at every node. A nonzero constant matrix
  `πI` of positive order has zero actual residue matrix, hence rank
  zero over the nontrivial residue quotient.
- **§3.3 actual scalar native observation.** The scalar `π+T`
  is observed in the actual two-level `LocalJet π P k` ring.
  Its multiplication image is the principal ideal of that
  observed element; the resulting scalar cokernel is identified
  as an `R`-algebra with `R/(π^(min P k))`. This uses both actual
  substitution maps and yields its exact `R`-module length under
  the stated nonzero-uniformizer and maximality hypotheses.
- **§3.3 failure of a Smith-style principal ideal.** The actual
  ideal `(2,z)` is not principal in `(ℤ/4)[z]/z²`. This is a concrete
  case of the stated nonprincipal double-truncation obstruction,
  proved by decoding into an actual square-zero extension and
  using the incompatible coordinate equations for a single generator.
  The concrete obstruction, not the alternative general computation
  of the cotangent-space dimension, is the formal counterexample here.
  The replay rebuilds the byte-identical pinned Mathlib implementation
  of `TrivSqZeroExt` as one support import, under its existing license.
- **§2.3 width of a quotient.** For any ideal of a PID, actual quotient
  factorization of width at most `w` is equivalent to having an
  integral lift of fraction-field rank at most `w`. The proof
  constructs integral factors from an actual Smith basis, proves
  field rank from the mapped nonzero diagonal submatrix, and lifts
  each entry of each quotient factor separately. The same minimum is
  attained on both sides. This includes every quotient by a power of
  the uniformizer in the paper's ordinary DVR setting; it is not a
  claim about constrained/algorithmically efficient lifts.
- **§6 recovered regression test.** `fixtures/check_attainable_main.py`
  is the byte-identical checker from the earlier research-v7 archive
  embedded in the independent C17 evidence. It is not a reconstructed
  test fitted to the paper's reported numbers. Its three Python 3
  standard-library runs reproduce the original 13 intervals/33
  nodes and global rank range 6–9. The independent script captures
  stderr separately and confirms every stdout line; in this replay
  even the historical and new stdout bytes are identical. Both outputs,
  the separate stderr and the source digests are retained. These
  finite experiments are not the proof of F.A1–F.A4.

## Evidence and boundaries

The new files are cold compiled by the exact pinned Lean 4.24.0 compiler
with warnings as errors. The additive source artifacts are separate
from the accepted public C17 and layout/window objects. The verification
script checks the exact published manifest identity, the 609 accepted
base rows, source digests of the five supplements, and all nine
dependency revisions; it builds fresh objects for the named overlay
modules only. Stdout, source and output object hashes are explicit.

- The public statements now indicated by `P5A2One`,
  `P5A2Simultaneous` and `P5A2SimulRank` already supply actual
  generic-field Hankel and double-truncated basis/rank proofs.
  Section 3's prose saying these were not separately checked is stale.
  The present overlay does not change that prose.
- **Explicit MAIN source extraction is a separate larger task, not a
  missing proof of a claimed end-to-end machine theorem.**
  `P5AlwaysSatOriginalTaylorFinite`, its sparse counterpart and
  `P5DirectSourceWindow` target the actual direct/sparse source
  coefficient recipes, genuine counts/appropriate parity and printed
  retained-order windows. `P5MainIteratorPaid.SourceVisits` gives three
  injections into the paid visits of one modeled call, while
  `P5MainIteratorScheduledSource.literalBoxSlots` is the analytic
  count of its declared full box. These are not a compiler extracting
  the full OpenAI TeX into a byte-level runner. Missing to make that
  stronger claim are a single executable initializer for the printed
  original layout metadata/order, a complete native MAIN iterator
  whose addressed loops and local coefficient/evaluation consumers
  agree with those same recipes at raw input, and an integrated metered
  bit-clock (including preprocessing, lookup and real arithmetic). The
  typed signed-unit trace is only for the stated source witness. Do not
  infer a global runtime theorem from the mere existence of an equal
  coefficient expansion, or require all optimized algorithms to pay
  for a printed loop they avoid. In the [pinned TeX](https://github.com/dch-and-Elara/some-research-inspired-by-openais-eth-preprint/blob/95ad3fb52aeac8376ba835b76987ef5f9d6156d6/rank-budget-barriers/paper/rank_budget_barriers.tex),
  §10 lines 2223–2244 explicitly give the trace/coverage premises,
  lines 2265–2274 exclude arbitrary implicit aggregation, and lines
  2291–2352 give the printed signed-unit reach and mandatory iterator
  argument. Lines 2599–2604 and 2794–2798 explicitly leave general
  source extraction as further work; lines 2822–2827 distinguish
  literal printed reach from the unsupported adaptive case.
- **D loop vs bit-cost:** `P5D1LiteralLedger` explicitly covers each
  slot of the root menu; it does not meter the internals of recursive
  contraction. The matching fixed-density bit upper bound stated in
  §11 is an external theorem of the source paper, not a new assumption
  or an upper bound proved by the present overlay.
- **E1:** The public exact same-layout offset and word-fee proofs use
  arbitrary legal sign-group functions and preindexed graph-only
  edge lists. This does not identify those functions with native MAIN
  jets or D descendants, or meter the entire graph/selector
  preprocessing bit by bit. The existence of an infinite simple cubic
  expander family remains an external MSS input; only source-family
  specialization, expansion from a supplied spectral gap, and the
  statements uniform over supplied graphs are Lean claims here.
- The full adaptive rational frontend remains outside the semantic
  extraction. The public parity, source row and reached iterator
  results do not imply unrestricted exact counting or SAT hardness.
