# Manuscript-to-Lean index

| Public manuscript statement | Lean declaration and hypotheses |
| --- | --- |
| Theorem 1, full-group lower bound | [RetunedLayout.modular_output_barrier](formal/RetunedLayout.lean) gives the two simultaneous finite inequalities. [FullGroupBarrier.Layout](formal/FullGroupBarrier.lean) declares the port count, collision inequalities, expansion, alphabet size and retained-coordinate conditions. [GroupProducts.GroupTerm](formal/GroupProducts.lean) gives the actual inputs of each local factor. |
| Theorem 2, balanced menus | [MenuBounds.source_distinct_menu_barrier](formal/MenuBounds.lean) treats a nonempty list of distinct coordinate/subset pairs. Each term is assigned its own eligible cut. The coefficient, logarithmic cutoff and menu scale used in the paper are defined in [FiniteGrowth](formal/FiniteGrowth.lean), [LogarithmicBudget](formal/LogarithmicBudget.lean) and [MenuBounds](formal/MenuBounds.lean). |
| Corollary 3, costs and polynomial comparison | [RetunedLayout.materialization_cost, superpolynomial_representation and superpolynomial_cost](formal/RetunedLayout.lean) prove the claims for the same certified layout. [FixedSchedule.Execution](formal/FixedSchedule.lean) specifies covers, event charges and total cost. [PowerCost](formal/PowerCost.lean) supplies the explicit cutoff. |
| From a layout to balanced local cuts | [SourceDensity](formal/SourceDensity.lean), [SourceCoordinates](formal/SourceCoordinates.lean) and [BalancedUnion](formal/BalancedUnion.lean) derive the projected density, coordinate cap and quarter-balanced unions. |
| One oriented induced matching for every term | [EmpiricalCutMoments](formal/EmpiricalCutMoments.lean) and [GraphCutMoments](formal/GraphCutMoments.lean) control the common signed second moment. [HuberEdgeCorrelation](formal/HuberEdgeCorrelation.lean) and [CommonMatchingSelection](formal/CommonMatchingSelection.lean) supply the common edge weights and the discrete matching. [ExpanderBarrier](formal/ExpanderBarrier.lean) composes the estimates. |
| Final output, field rank and global zero test | [PinnedCNF](formal/PinnedCNF.lean) proves the model-count semantics and matching restriction, as well as the alternative global zero test. [SeparatedRank](formal/SeparatedRank.lean) bounds individual term ranks. [IntegralOutputTransfer](formal/IntegralOutputTransfer.lean) transfers nonvanishing of the determinant to the field. |

The [verification record](verification/result.txt) covers all included Lean modules. The three numbered results quantify over layouts and output representations satisfying the displayed hypotheses. The specific SAT implementation, its Hermite schedule, a matching upper bound and native Taylor coefficient hardness are outside this public edition.
