| One oriented induced matching for every term | [EmpiricalCutMoments](formal/EmpiricalCutMoments.lean) and [GraphCutMoments](formal/GraphCutMoments.lean) control the common signed second moment. | Section 3 and Appendix A, balanced tests | [ProductVariance](formal/ProductVariance.lean) and [EmpiricalCutMoments](formal/EmpiricalCutMoments.lean) bound the signed second moment under the pin-label law. [HuberPotential](formal/HuberPotential.lean) supplies the pointwise remainder. [HuberCorrelation.exists_simultaneous_correlation](formal/HuberCorrelation.lean) gives the single bounded function with constant 3/16. The paper also retains the original smooth proof of a weaker consequence. |
| Appendix B and Section 4, edge tests that may vanish | [GraphCutMoments.cubic_expander_cut_correlations](formal/GraphCutMoments.lean) and [HuberEdgeCorrelation.exists_edge_correlation_of_doubled_bound](formal/HuberEdgeCorrelation.lean) give the rational edge estimate in (B1)–(B2). The finite graph proof uses this estimate. Nazarov’s real-variable majorant and the classical flow interpretation provide background. |
| Section 5, finite size and rounding | [FiniteGrowth](formal/FiniteGrowth.lean) proves the strict selection budget and floor bound. [LogarithmicBudget](formal/LogarithmicBudget.lean) gives the log-square cutoff. [PowerCost](formal/PowerCost.lean) compares the exponent with each fixed polynomial. |
| Section 6, weighted lifting and truncated jets | [IntegralOutputTransfer.weighted_lift_identity](formal/IntegralOutputTransfer.lean) gives the exact integral error after clearing denominators. [NativeJetEvaluation.eval_truncated_at_precision](formal/NativeJetEvaluation.lean) evaluates a truncated jet at a nilpotent point. |
| Section 7, overlap, adaptivity and the global computation | [OverlappingRank.overlap_weighted](formal/OverlappingRank.lean) proves (F) and the uniform leakage bound. [GuardedBarrier.tree_barrier](formal/GuardedBarrier.lean) gives (H) with the path predicates realized in each term’s two views. [PinnedCNF.count_eq_rational_product](formal/PinnedCNF.lean) proves (I). |
